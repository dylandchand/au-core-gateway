Test run on made-up patients

Can a pathology lab's patient data be trusted in Australia's national health record format?

This page answers that for one test run. A converter called the AU Core Gateway reads the messages a lab sends, translates each one into the national format, and has the official checker test the result. Wherever something fails, the page shows the exact spot in the lab's original message.

The problem

Most hospital and laboratory systems in Australia still send patient records in an older text format, . The national standard for sharing records digitally is , built on , and the Australian Digital Health Agency now requires FHIR connections to to meet it.

Translating between the two is where things go wrong. A mistyped digit in an , a birth date written day-first, or a blood test filed against the wrong person can make a record unusable, or attach it to someone else.
